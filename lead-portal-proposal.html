PROPOSAL · PARTNER PROGRAM

The Lead Portal

A branded self‑serve home for prospective partners — where leads from our ads register, get nurtured with announcements and webinars, and book their "become a partner" call. When they convert, the same login elevates into the full Partner Portal.

The problem

We generate strong lead flow, but too much of it stalls.

😕 Leads go into limbo

We run FB / Google / LinkedIn ads and hold meetings, but between touchpoints leads have nowhere to go and nothing pulling them forward. Follow‑up is manual and inconsistent.

🧩 Scattered nurture

Webinars, events, and updates live in different places. There's no single, branded home that keeps a prospect warm and moving toward becoming a partner.

The solution

A lightweight "Partner Program" portal — the front door of the partner journey.

🚪 One identity, that graduates

A lead registers once (verified email & phone). Same login and credentials carry through — when they're approved, their access simply elevates to full Partner. No second account, no migration.

🎯 A reason to keep coming back

Announcements, event invites, and on‑demand webinars give leads real value between meetings — which is exactly what keeps them from going cold.

What's in the Lead Portal

Focused and simple — three things.

event_available

Book‑a‑Call Calendar

When a lead feels ready to become a partner, they book directly — the meeting flows into HighLevel and their assigned rep.

campaign

Announcements & Events

Targeted announcements for upcoming events and program news — powered by our existing marketing engine.

play_circle

Webinars & Videos

One home for our scattered webinars and training videos — and it stays available after they convert to a partner.

How the journey works

From ad click to onboarded partner — one continuous path.

1

Opt in

Ad → register on the portal (verified email & phone) → GHL contact created.

2

Nurture

Lead gets announcements, newsletters (via GHL), and webinars — staying warm.

3

Book

When ready, the lead books the "become a partner" call with their rep.

4

Convert

On approval, access elevates — the same login opens the full Partner Portal.

How it fits what we already run

HighLevel stays the system of record. The portal is the journey layer on top.

AreaApproach
CommunicationsAll comms & newsletters stay in HighLevel — the portal doesn't replace them.
Lead ownershipReps keep working leads in HighLevel; the portal mirrors the assigned rep and booking.
Leads stay separateLeads never appear in the partner dashboard or partner data — they're isolated until they convert.
ConversionStaff (or a GHL pipeline stage) marks a lead approved → access elevates to full Partner.
BrandingA warm, aspirational "PayProTec Partner Program" that becomes the Partner Portal on conversion.

Suggested rollout

Ship value fast, integrate deeper over time.

Phase 1

Stand it up

  • Registration (verified email/phone)
  • Book‑a‑call calendar
  • Announcements + webinars
  • Creates the GHL contact
Phase 2

Graduate to partner

  • Staff "Convert to partner" action
  • Same login → full portal access
  • Webinars carry over
Phase 3

Deepen GHL sync

  • Assigned‑rep sync
  • Pipeline stage ⇄ portal status
  • Conversion driven from GHL

Why now

  • Turns paid ad spend into a warm, self‑serve pipeline instead of leads in limbo.
  • Reuses infrastructure we already built — announcements, GHL integration, partner auth.
  • Gives reps visibility and leverage without changing how they work in HighLevel.
  • One seamless identity from prospect → partner.
